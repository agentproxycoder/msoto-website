Text Message Privacy Policy

How MSO handles information collected through the text message alert program.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how MSO, 1211 Cathedral St, Baltimore, MD 21201, handles information collected through the MSO text message alert program.

Information we collect

When you sign up, we collect your first and last name, email address, mobile phone number, designation and specialty, along with a record of your consent to receive text messages.

How we use it

We use your information to send you MSO text alerts about events, deadlines and member news, to manage your subscription, and to keep records of your consent and opt-out requests.

No sharing for marketing

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Service providers

We use service providers, such as our messaging platform and mobile carriers, only to deliver messages on our behalf. They may use your information only to provide that service.

Legal requirements

We may disclose information when required by law.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any MSO text message to unsubscribe at any time, or reply HELP for help. To update or delete your information, write to MSO at the address above.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information.

Age

The MSO text message program is intended for adults 18 and older.

Changes

MSO may update this policy at any time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.

Terms

See the MSO Text Message Terms & Conditions.
