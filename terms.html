Text Message Terms & Conditions

The terms for the MSO text message alert program.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Program description

MSO sends recurring text messages about upcoming MSO events, deadlines and member news to people who sign up through the MSO text sign-up form.

How you opt in

You opt in by entering your mobile number on the MSO text sign-up form and clicking Sign Up. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of membership or of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any MSO text message to cancel at any time. You will receive one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. To rejoin, sign up again on the MSO text sign-up form.

Help

Reply HELP to any MSO text message for help, or write to MSO, 1211 Cathedral St, Baltimore, MD 21201.

Carrier liability

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and the account holder of the mobile number you provide, or have the account holder's permission to enroll it.

Privacy

Your mobile number and text-messaging consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. See the MSO Text Message Privacy Policy.

Changes

MSO may update these terms at any time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
