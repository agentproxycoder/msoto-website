Advocacy

Protecting physician-led ENT care in Maryland

MSO speaks for Maryland otolaryngologists before the General Assembly, regulators and insurers, so patients get care from the right professional with the right training.

Maryland legislative priorities

What MSO is focused on heading into the 2027 Maryland General Assembly session.

01

Scope of practice: keep ENT care physician-led

MSO opposes legislation that would let non-physician providers practice beyond their education and training, including proposals for independent diagnosis, treatment or medical management of ear, nose and throat disorders. Maryland expanded the scope of practice for audiologists in 2024. In 2026, MedChi supported SB 917 to clarify the legal definition of audiology practice. MSO will keep watching for new scope proposals in the 2027 session.

02

A physician-led hearing health care team

Audiologists and hearing professionals are valued partners. MSO supports a team model in which patients with medical ear conditions are evaluated by a physician.

03

Fair payment and insurer accountability

MSO joins MedChi in pressing insurers on downcoding, prior authorization and network adequacy, and in protecting Medicaid payment rates for office visits.

04

A stable medical liability environment

MSO opposes bills that would repeal or raise Maryland’s cap on non-economic damages, which helps keep physicians practicing in the state.
