About MSO

Uniting Maryland otolaryngologists since 1977

The Maryland Society of Otolaryngologists is a nonprofit, nonpartisan association of Maryland otolaryngologists, established and governed by its members.

Our mission

  1. Promote the shared professional interests of Maryland otolaryngologists and otolaryngology residents.
  2. Advance and protect the welfare of patients receiving otolaryngologic care.
  3. Educate Maryland citizens, policymakers and legislators on issues affecting the practice of otolaryngology.
  4. Acquire, preserve, exchange and disseminate knowledge related to the science and practice of otolaryngology.
  5. Foster communication, collaboration and cooperation with professional organizations and other stakeholders within and outside Maryland.
  6. Promote collegiality and professional engagement through educational, networking and social activities.
  7. Represent the collective interests of its members before governmental bodies, healthcare organizations, professional societies and the public as the unified voice of Maryland otolaryngologists.
